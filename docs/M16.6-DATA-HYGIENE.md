# ReleaseCore M16.6 — Data Hygiene & Identity Maintenance

M16.6 adds a merchant-visible maintenance system for local ReleaseCore catalog integrity.

## Data Maintenance

The new `/app/data-hygiene` workspace is linked from Settings and provides duplicate Artist and Contributor candidates, Artist ↔ Contributor identity matches, unused-record detection, release artist-name cache drift repair, local Shopify-link consistency checks, cross-tenant relationship diagnostics, and maintenance audit history.

The scanner is diagnostic. It does not automatically delete, merge, or rewrite records.

## Artist merge

Artist → Artist merge runs inside a Prisma transaction and migrates or deduplicates ReleaseArtist, TrackArtist, PortalArtistAccess, PortalCustomerPolicy.soloArtistId, and ArtistContributor relationships. The destination Artist wins on conflicting profile fields; empty destination fields are filled from the source. A Shopify collection conflict requires an explicit administrator choice, and the losing Shopify collection is left untouched in Shopify.

## Contributor merge

Contributor → Contributor merge moves TrackCredit and ArtistContributor relationships. If the same track/role exists on both records with different ownership percentages, the merge cannot proceed until the administrator explicitly keeps the destination value, uses the source value, or enters a corrected percentage.

## Artist ↔ Contributor identity

Artist and Contributor remain separate role-specific models. `ArtistContributor.relationshipType` distinguishes `REGULAR` relationships from `SAME_PERSON` identity links.

## Safe cleanup

Unused Artist deletion requires zero release, track, portal-access, solo-policy, contributor, and Shopify collection relationships. Unused Contributor deletion requires zero credits, zero Artist links, and no owner customer relationship. Shopify files and collections are never automatically deleted.

## Audit

`DataMaintenanceEvent` records maintenance operations scoped by shop and deployment profile. It stores identifiers, a safe summary, and minimal structured details without staff identity, request headers, IP addresses, or secrets.
